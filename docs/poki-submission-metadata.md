# Poki submission metadata draft

**Game title:** Night Market: Street Food Stall

**Short description:** Run a street food stall in a lively night market. Prepare noodle dishes, serve hungry customers, and upgrade your stall as you progress through six days.

**Long description:** Take orders and cook street food across a six-day night market campaign. Add ingredients to the griddle, manage the heat, spread sauce, cut and roll each dish, then pack it for your waiting customers. Earn coins from completed orders and spend them on stall upgrades as new ingredients and recipes become available.

**Controls:** Mouse / Touch. Tap or click ingredients to add them, or drag them to a griddle. Use gestures to spread sauce, cut and roll, then select the finished dish to pack and serve. Keyboard controls are available where applicable: Enter / Space activate focused buttons; Escape opens Pause, H opens help, and M toggles music during gameplay.

**Gameplay genre:** Cooking / Management / Simulation

**Orientation:** Landscape

**Language:** English gameplay UI

**Supported input:** Mouse; Touch; Keyboard where applicable

**Candidate tags:** Cooking, Food, Restaurant, Management, Simulation
